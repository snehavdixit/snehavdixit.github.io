My Beamer Themes

Since the University of Nebraska-Lincoln does not have an official Beamer theme, I created one myself. You will never, ever catch me using powerpoint with slab-serif fonts for any of my presentations! This theme includes a footer with the UNL logo, and also a progress bar indicative of slide number :) I am quite proud of how sleek the theme is, while retaining the visual language of UNL.
